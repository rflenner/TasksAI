# Close Plan — features

Plain description of what the product does today, for positioning work. Status: **Live** = working now, **Paused** = built or agreed but switched off until the test round, **Not yet** = not built.

## Build the plan
- **Start from the deal.** Search the opportunity (account, amount, stage, close date, contacts) or enter it by hand. Live.
- **Templates.** Standard B2B, POC-led, Enterprise procurement, Renewal & expansion, App design & build, Product MVP with a development partner, Blank. Live.
- **Dates fitted to the real timeline.** Template dates are stretched between plan start, target signature and target go-live. Live.
- **Roles, not names, in templates.** Tasks are written for roles (executive sponsor, security, procurement, legal, solutions engineer…) and assigned to people in the guide. Live.
- **Draft first.** Nothing is shared or sent until the owner invites people. A "Getting started" checklist guides the first steps. Live.
- **Import from Salesforce.** Create a plan from an opportunity's existing phases and checkpoints. Live.

## Plan structure
- **Phases** with a goal and dates; they can overlap; only plan owners edit them. Live.
- **Tasks and subtasks** with owner, coworkers, requested by, due date, status, description, checklist, updates. Live.
- **Milestones**, including target signature and target go-live, which drive the plan header and PDF. Live.
- **Internal tasks** hidden from the customer. Live.
- **Timeline** (single lane, or Gantt when phases overlap) and **progress bars** per phase. Live.
- **Folding phases** so finished and future phases stay out of the way. Live.
- **Drag to reorder** tasks and move them between phases. Live.
- **Status moves on its own** when work starts (an update, a started subtask). Live.
- **Next up** panel and **Recent activity** with a 7-day summary (AI-written for the iSEEit team, rule-based for customers). Live.

## Two sides, one plan
- **Two plan owners:** one seller, one buyer. Live.
- **People tab** with each person's open, due and overdue items. Live.
- **Per-person access:** view only, own tasks, all tasks; can create tasks or not. Live.
- **Customer contacts picked from the CRM** (with suggested roles from job titles), or added by hand. Live.
- **Personal links for customers:** no account, no password, valid 60 days, can be switched off any time. Live.
- **Customers can** update status, post updates, close their tasks, add tasks and subtasks, and download the PDF. Live.
- **Customers never see** internal tasks, CRM deal data or iSEEit email addresses. Live.

## Sharing and output
- **Client PDF report:** where we stand, key dates, timeline, next steps, every phase with shared tasks, both teams. Live.
- **Link preview** with the plan's name and logo when a link is pasted into chat or email. Live.
- **Custom address** (closeplan.iseeit.ai). Setup.

## Communication
- **Invitation email** with the personal link; reminder if not opened. Invitation live; reminders Paused.
- **Task-assigned notices** by email, and Slack for iSEEit people. Live.
- **"Added to a plan" notices** for iSEEit people. Live.
- **Monday email:** overview for owners, own tasks for others, nothing if nothing is open. Paused.
- **Overdue reminders.** Paused for customers; shown in the Monday email once active.
- **Weekly cap:** at most one scheduled email per customer per plan per week. Live.
- **Admin rules** for who gets what, and an **opt-out list** plus an unsubscribe link in every email. Live.
- **Reply by email** to update a task. Not yet (deferred).

## Voice assistant (iSEEit team)
- Ask where the deal stands, what to do next, what's overdue, what we're waiting on from the customer. Live.
- Open, update, close, move, reassign or re-date tasks, and add tasks and subtasks by voice, with a short follow-up conversation for missing details. Live.
- Confirmation by voice before closing a task or moving a milestone. Live.
- "What can I say?" help, and a backlog of requests it couldn't handle. Live.
- Not available to customers. Not yet.

## Salesforce
- **Two-way sync** of phases, tasks, status, due dates, owners, order and sharing with the iSEEit close plan objects. Live.
- **Preview before applying;** automatic check when a plan is opened and write-back after each save, once linked. Live.
- **Conflicts:** Close Plan wins, and the overwritten Salesforce value is noted in the activity. Live.
- **Subtasks** sync to Salesforce. Not yet.

## Admin and trust
- Roles: Site Admin, Area Admin, plan owner, member, customer contact. Live.
- Every task and plan change is recorded in the activity. Live.
- Internal and customer views are separated on the server, not just hidden on screen. Live.

## Not yet / on the roadmap
- Isolated workspaces per customer company.
- Save a plan as your own template; company-managed templates.
- Customer-side voice assistant.
- Stage and close-date drift notices from the CRM.
- Reply-by-email from digests.
